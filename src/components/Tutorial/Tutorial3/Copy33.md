As we’ve stated again and again, all signals in `AudioCanvas` - without exception - are streams of numbers.  

This means you can always plug any output into any input or parameter, just to see what happens.  

Whether the effect will be interesting is another matter of course, but when playings with audio synthesis - especially in a modular environment like `AudioCanvas` - never be afraid to try unusual things.
