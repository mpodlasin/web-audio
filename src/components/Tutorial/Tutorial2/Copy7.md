You can now see that the number displayed in `Value` gradually moves up and down between the values of -1 and 1.

Perhaps you've seen how a spaker cone moves forwards and backwards when playing sounds.

The numbers generated by `Sine` oscillator are representing the position of the cone.

Number 0 would represent the cone being at rest position.

1 would signify the cone being protracted forward as much as physically possible.

-1 would signify the cone being retracted backward as much as possible.
