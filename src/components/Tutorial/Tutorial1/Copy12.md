You can now see the "shape" of a sine wave.

What that means is that this is the visualization of the movement that the membranes of your speaker make when generating this sound.

On `y` (or vertical) axis you can see the position of the speaker membrane, while on `x` (horizontal) axis you can see time.

By looking at the oscilloscope we see that speaker fluctuates back and forth. Not only that, it does it in a characteristic, smooth manner - we don't see any sharp eges or anything like that.
