Now we need to connect the <Clr nodeType="sine-oscillator">Sine</Clr> oscillator to the <Clr nodeType="destination">Output</Clr> node, just like connecting a guitar to an amplifier with a cable.

To do this, find the small circle at the bottom right corner of the <Clr nodeType="sine-oscillator">Sine</Clr> oscillator.

Click and hold it, then drag a white line to the similar circle on the lower left corner of the <Clr nodeType="destination">Output</Clr> node.
