This is a `Gain` node.

It can be used to adjust the volume of a sound.

Now we need to connect the `Sine` oscillator to the `Gain` node, just as we would connect a guitar to an amplifier with a cable.

To do that, find a little circle at the bottom of the `Sine` oscillator, click and hold it, and drag a white "cable" to the similar circle in the lower left (not right!) corner of the `Gain` node.
