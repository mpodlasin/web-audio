It turns out that nothing stops us from connecting the output of the <Clr nodeType="sequencer">Sequencer</Clr> directly to the frequency knob!

Yup, you've read that right. Start dragging a line from the output of the <Clr nodeType="sequencer">Sequencer</Clr> and place it over the frequency knob on the <Clr nodeType="sine-oscillator">Sine</Clr> node.

Once you let go, you will see the line snap to the frequency knob.
