This is a sine oscillator.

It is a sound source that generates a pure sine wave.

We will explain what exactly that means in later tutorials, but for now let's just hear how it sounds.

Before we can direct it to your speakers however, we need to adjust the volume of the sound generated by oscillator, to protect your speakers and ears.

To do that, we need to create another node - a `Gain` node.

Click or tap on the background of the app and select `Gain > Gain` from the context menu.
