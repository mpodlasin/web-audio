This is a sine wave oscillator.

It is a sound source that generates a pure sine wave.

We will explain what exactly that means in later tutorials, but for now, let's just hear how it sounds.

To do that, we need to route the audio signal to your headphones or speakers.

The node responsible for that is called <Clr nodeType="destination">Output</Clr>. You can find it in the <Clr nodeType="destination">I/O</Clr> section of the node creation menu.
