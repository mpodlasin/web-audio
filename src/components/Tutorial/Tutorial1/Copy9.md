What you are hearing now is a sine wave from our `Sine` oscillator, with volume reduced using `Gain`.

You can see now that we can combine nodes with different functions in order to achive auditory effects.

Listen to the tone generated by your computer.

It may sound to you a bit unnatural, synthetic, but also pure and clean.

That's not an accident. In later tutorials we will learn that sine wave is a building block of all sounds.

However listening to constant sine wave can becoming tiring fast.

To make it less boring, play around with the frequency knob on `Sine` node and listen how it changes the sound.
