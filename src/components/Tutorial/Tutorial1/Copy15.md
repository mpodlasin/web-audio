Now if you press play at the top left corner of the app, you will hear a "melody" playing.

But you might feel a bit underwhelmed. The "melody" probably doesn't sound too melodic at all.

That's because western music scales - the ones our ears are used to - consist of very specific frequency values.

Because our frequency values are more or less random, the melody probably does not sound too pleasing to your ear.

There is however a way to force frequency values from `Sequencer` to "fit" notes of typical western scale.

We can use a `Pitch Quantizer` node - found in `Pitch` section.
