Now that the `Sine` oscillator is connected to the `Gain` node, an audio signal can flow between them.

All nodes in `AudioCanvas` have signal inputs and/or outputs - depending on their function.

Inputs are always on the left and outputs are always on the right of a node.

So now we can lower the volume of the `Sine` oscillator by using the large knob at the center of the `Gain` node.

Click on the gain knob and drag your mouse down, until the number under the knob is exactly 0.
