And that's it!

You've built a small, simple modular synthesizer that can play melodies.

We encourage you to play around with this setup a little more:

- Change frequency parameters in `Sequencer` node to generate different frequencies.
- Change notes selected in `Pitch Quantizer` to adjust those frequencies to different scales (if you don't know music theory, for now simply stick to notes without the `#` sign - this way everything will always sound melodic).
- Change tempo of `Clock` for your melody to be either slower or higher.
- After that adjust attack and decay values of `AD Envelope` node to fit best to the tempo you have chosen.
- Set reverb in `Convolution` node to desirable value. For faster melodies less reverb might be good, for slower the reverse.

Remember that there are no "correct" values for parameters above (and any others for that matter). It all depends on your own musical taste and preferences.

So relax and have fun. Just don't bother your neighbours too much!
